## Planetary Health Check 2026

`PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf`: _Planetary Health Check 2026: A Scientific Assessment of the State of the Planet_.

- **Authors:** Levke Caesar and Boris Sakschewski (co-first authors), with Lauren Seaby Andersen, Johan Rockström and 60+ co-authors. Editors: Niklas H. Kitzmann, Boris Sakschewski, Levke Caesar, Johan Rockström.
- **Publisher:** Planetary Boundaries Science (PBScience), Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), 2026.
- **DOI:** [10.48485/pik.2026.25](https://doi.org/10.48485/pik.2026.25). More at [planetaryhealthcheck.org](https://planetaryhealthcheck.org).
- **Licence:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), "unless otherwise credited". The photographs (Mattias Klum, Maxar Technologies) and the art and figures (Globaïa) are credited separately, so they are not reused on the site.
- **Summary:** The report assesses the nine Planetary Boundaries, the processes that keep Earth in a Holocene-like state, through 13 control variables. It finds seven of the nine transgressed in 2026: climate change, biosphere integrity, land system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows, ocean acidification (for the first time) and novel entities. Aerosol loading is within its boundary globally, though over a proposed regional boundary in South Asia. Stratospheric ozone is within its boundary. Its spotlights are the weakening land carbon sink, the seafloor, and chemical pollution.
